# Lot 8 results — fastgrpc3 on both transports, 30/10/2026

fastgrpc's `benches/e2e_bench.py` (unary echo; each server alone in its process, pinned to
CPU 0; `ghz` on CPUs 2–7 with pre-encoded requests; 5 s per point), on an AMD Ryzen 7 PRO
7840U (laptop on mains, `powersave` governor, otherwise idle). fastgrpc3 0.1.0.dev0,
fastgrpc3-transport 0.1.0, protoruf 0.5.3, grpcio 1.85.0, pydantic 2.14.1, fastgrpc2 0.5.0.

Two crossed passes, averaged: A ran the `grpc.aio` servers then fastgrpc3 on tonic
(`FASTGRPC_TRANSPORT=tonic`); B ran them in the reverse order. A and B differ by 2 % at
most for tonic, by up to 11 % for the `grpc.aio` servers.

## Reading

- Small and medium messages: fastgrpc3 on tonic is 7.6× / 9.8× fastgrpc3 on `grpc.aio`
  (small, c=16 / c=64) and 5.9× / 7.4× (medium); 9.1× plain `grpc.aio` without any
  framework and 10.4× fastgrpc2 (small, c=64); 1.6–2.0× with one client.
- Large messages (23 KB, about 1,000 nested objects): 1.2–1.3×; the time goes into
  building and validating the Python objects, not into the transport (`validate_requests=
  False` gives +34 % on tonic, +30 % on `grpc.aio`).
- A first, single pass run while the machine was busy gave tonic numbers up to 20 % lower
  and an inverted "no validation" row; compare rows measured together only.
