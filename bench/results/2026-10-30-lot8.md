# Lot 8 results — fastgrpc3 on both transports, 30/10/2026

fastgrpc's `benches/e2e_bench.py` (unary echo; each server alone in its process, pinned to
CPU 0; `ghz` on CPUs 2–7 with pre-encoded requests; 5 s per point), on an AMD Ryzen 7 PRO
7840U (laptop on mains, `powersave` governor). fastgrpc3 0.1.0.dev0, fastgrpc3-transport
0.1.0, protoruf 0.5.3, grpcio 1.85.0, pydantic 2.14.1, fastgrpc2 0.5.0. One pass, in this
order: the `grpc.aio` servers, then fastgrpc3 on tonic (`FASTGRPC_TRANSPORT=tonic`).

## Reading

- Small and medium messages: fastgrpc3 on tonic is 8.0× / 10.3× fastgrpc3 on `grpc.aio`
  (small, c=16 / c=64) and 5.9× / 7.4× (medium); 9.6× plain `grpc.aio` without framework
  (small, c=64); 1.6–1.9× with one client.
- Large messages (23 KB, about 1,000 nested objects): about 1.2×; the time goes into
  building and validating the Python objects, not into the transport.
- The tonic "no validation" row ran last, while the laptop was heating: run again in the
  reverse order (small and medium only), "no validation" is ahead (36,246 vs 34,193 at
  small c=64; 24,887 vs 23,742 at medium c=64) but both 15–20 % lower than the first pass.
  Compare rows of one pass only.
