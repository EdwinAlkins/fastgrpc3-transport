# Lot 4 results — fastgrpc3 on tonic, 30/10/2026

Same machine and method as `2026-10-30.md`, but the server is now the real fastgrpc3 app
(`fastgrpc/benches/e2e_bench.py --modes fastgrpc3`: `Invoker`, Pydantic validation,
`MessageCodec`), run on each transport with `FASTGRPC_TRANSPORT`. Server on one core, `ghz`
on 2-7, 4 s per point, two interleaved passes.

## Unary, requests/s

| transport | small c=1 | small c=16 | small c=64 | medium c=1 | medium c=16 | medium c=64 |

fastgrpc3 on tonic: **8.0× / 10.4×** `grpc.aio` on the small message at c=16 / c=64,
**6.0× / 7.7×** on the medium one, ~2× at c=1. Close to the bare variant B of lot 2
(medium 24,370–25,957 / 30,955–31,897): the shared `Invoker` costs little.
